Why use the scheduler?

Everything organized around your publishing workflow.

Prepare content, choose the Pages that should receive it, and keep an eye on what is scheduled or already processed.

▦

Bulk Pages

Select the Pages you want for a post, add your caption and photos, then publish now or schedule each selected Page.

▧

Bulk Photos

Choose one Page and prepare up to 10 separate post slots. Each slot supports its own caption, schedule time and up to 10 photos.

◷

History at a glance

Review scheduled, published and failed jobs. Clearing app history does not delete posts already published to Facebook.

⏱

Plan-aware scheduling

Scheduling is limited by your plan and the time remaining before your subscription expires—whichever gives the shorter window.

✦

AI image disclosure

For posts marked as AI-generated, the scheduler adds the disclosure line to the end of the caption.

🔐

Customer account

Your scheduler account is separate from Facebook. Page access still requires a valid Facebook connection and granted permissions.

User manual

Three simple steps to get started.

These guides explain the workflow. Real screenshots from the deployed scheduler will be added after the corresponding live screens are captured and verified.

Guide 01 · Account setup

Create an account and connect Facebook

  1. Open the scheduler workspace.
  2. Create or sign in to your customer account.
  3. Choose Connect with Facebook and review the requested access.
  4. Load and select the Pages you manage.
Guide 02 · One Page

Prepare a Bulk Photos batch

  1. Choose one connected Page.
  2. Add posts and upload 1–10 photos per post.
  3. Enter captions and schedule times for each slot.
  4. Review the batch and submit the schedule.
Guide 03 · Multiple Pages

Publish or schedule with Bulk Pages

  1. Open Bulk Pages and select target Pages.
  2. Add your caption and photos.
  3. Choose Publish Now or Schedule.
  4. Check History to review the job status.
