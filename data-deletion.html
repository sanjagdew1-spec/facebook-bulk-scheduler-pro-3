User Data Deletion

Facebook Bulk Scheduler Pro 3
Last updated: October 17, 2026

Pre-release status: The software owner must publish a working support email or other reliable contact method and verify the deletion workflow before accepting public customers. Do not submit real customer data to this test pilot.

What can be included in a deletion request?

A request may cover your scheduler account, Facebook connection records and stored access tokens, Page identifiers and names, scheduled or published job history, captions, uploaded images, and related session records.

How to request deletion

Before public launch, the owner will publish a support contact here. Once that contact is available, include the email address associated with your scheduler account and enough information to identify the account. Do not send your Facebook password, access tokens, or Meta app secrets.

What happens next?

The owner should verify the request, disable the account and revoke or delete stored Facebook connection data, remove applicable Page and job records, and confirm when deletion is complete. Any legal retention exception should be explained to the requester. Deletion from the scheduler does not delete content already published to Facebook; manage that content through Facebook.

Revoke Facebook access

You can also remove the application's permissions from your Facebook account settings. This helps stop future access but does not, by itself, guarantee deletion of data already stored by the scheduler.

Current pilot limitation

This Phase 2 build is still under testing. The public-facing deletion contact and an end-to-end account deletion feature are not yet verified. This page must be completed and the deletion process tested before the service is offered to customers.
