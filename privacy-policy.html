Privacy Policy

Facebook Bulk Scheduler Pro 3
Last updated: October 17, 2026

Pre-release policy: A support contact must be added by the software owner before this service is offered publicly. This Phase 2 pilot is still under testing and must not be used for sensitive or critical data.

Information the service handles

  • Scheduler account details, including your email address and a password verifier. The service is designed not to store your plain-text password.
  • Facebook account identifiers and display name returned during Facebook Login.
  • Facebook Page identifiers, Page names, permissions needed for the features you request, and access tokens needed to connect and publish. Facebook tokens are encrypted in the application database.
  • Content you choose to upload or schedule, including captions, images, scheduled times, publishing status, and error/history details.
  • Session and technical request information needed to sign you in, protect the service, and troubleshoot failures.

How information is used

Information is used to operate customer accounts, connect the Facebook account you choose, display your Pages, publish or schedule the content you request, show publishing history, maintain security, and diagnose errors. We do not sell Facebook user data or use it for unrelated advertising.

Facebook and third-party services

Facebook Login and publishing are subject to Meta's terms, permissions, platform policies, and availability. When you connect Facebook, information is exchanged with Meta to perform the actions you request. Render may process application data as the hosting provider. Their respective policies also apply.

Security and retention

The application uses password hashing, server-side sessions, customer-scoped data access, and encryption for stored Facebook access tokens. These measures reduce risk but cannot guarantee absolute security. The free testing environment may lose stored data when its service restarts, sleeps, or is redeployed; it is not suitable for critical records or guaranteed scheduled publishing.

During the pilot, data is retained to provide the service and show scheduled jobs/history. You may revoke the app's Facebook permissions in Facebook settings. The owner must provide a working support contact and a tested deletion process before public launch so users can request deletion of scheduler account data, connected tokens, Pages, images, and job history.

Your choices

  • Connect only the Facebook account and Pages you want to use with the scheduler.
  • Revoke Facebook permissions through Facebook settings to stop future access.
  • Do not use the pre-release pilot for sensitive or business-critical information.

Children

This service is intended for people who are permitted to manage Facebook Pages and use the service under applicable laws and Meta's terms. It is not designed for children.

Policy changes and contact

This policy may be updated as the service develops. A public support email or other reliable contact method must be published here before customers are onboarded. Until then, this page is a pre-release draft and the service is not ready for public customer use.
